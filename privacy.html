Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

"My n8n Automation" is a personal automation app. It connects my own Google account to my own n8n instance so I can automate my own tasks.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the Google data needed for the workflows I build (for example, Google Sheets, Drive, Gmail or Calendar), and only for my own account.

How data is used

Data is used solely to run my personal automations. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored in my n8n instance. I do not keep copies of Google data beyond what my workflows need.
